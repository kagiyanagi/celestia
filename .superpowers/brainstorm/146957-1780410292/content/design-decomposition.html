The "Tiger Style" Constraint

  • Logic Separation: All complex "if/else" and data sorting logic will move out of the JSX and into small, testable helper functions.
  • Function Depth: No sub-component will exceed 70-100 lines of UI code.
  • Prop Safety: Using TypeScript interfaces to ensure components only receive exactly what they need, reducing "prop drilling".
